## Overview

### Purpose

The Cal Poly Food Pantry provides students with free access to essential food resources. Only problem is you never know what their stock currently is until you get there. Our goal is to make it easier for students to see what items are available ahead of time and request specific items they would like the pantry to carry.

The PolyPantry aims to improve visibility into pantry inventory, help students find the items they need, and provide a way for the students to communicate their needs and preferences.
